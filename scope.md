# Project Scope

## Project Purpose

The purpose of this project is to create a personal webpage that gives people a better insight into my growth within coding, Markdown, and overall just my technological journey. But also to show my journey and how I can take what I learn and apply it to my future career. I also want to use this project as an opportunity to practice working with Markdown and HTML, because both are useful skills for building websites and also creating content on websites and organizing them.

## Description

For this project, I will create two files: `scope.md` and `index.html`.

The `scope.md` file pretty much explains the overall purpose of this project. The `index.html` file will be a simple personal webpage about me. It will include my name, Isaac Ihebom, my major in Computer Applications and Legal Studies, and information about my career interests and hobbies. So almost like a personal portfolio or a resume.

The webpage will also talk about some of my goals in my life and how I ccan achieve them. My main goal is to graduate this spring semester and begin building a career in cybersecurity. After graduation, I would like to either work in a cybersecurity position or join the Air Force and join their cybersecurity so I can build on that and gain benefits for my future family. I hope to kock out a couple of years just building my skills and gaining real-world experience, so eventually I will be able to work in the FBI.

On the other side of my education and career choices, my webpage will also talk about a few of my hobbies, which are fishing, playing guitar, and hunting. It will also include a link to my GitHub profile, where I can share my projects.

## Desired Results

When this project is finished, I want to have a clean, organized, and easy-to-read website that shows who I am and my growth within this semester. Someone visiting the page should be able to quickly get to know me virtually without actually meeting me. The users are able to learn my name, what I am studying, what I enjoy doing, and what I hope to accomplish in my career. The final project should meet all of the assignment requirements while still sounding personal and natural rather than overly formal or performative.

## Exclusions

This project is meant to focus on the basic structure of your own webpage, so I will not be adding all the fancy features. The project will not include things such as accounts/profiles, online payments, complex animations, or interactive features. For now, the goal is to create one simple webpage that introduces me and emphasizes my understanding of basic HTML skills, and also show a clear Markdown document that explains the scope of the project. So, it won’t be a full professional website by any means, but it also won’t be as basic.
